Session 1: Fine-tune a model

Everyone does this on their own. One GPU each.

01

Log in to the supercomputer portal

Go to morehouse.tapis.io and click Log in with single sign-on. Enter your TACC username, password, and the code from your authenticator app.

02

Connect your account to Vista

Click Systems in the left panel and find the Vista system. It will say you are not yet authenticated.

Click Authenticate with TMS Keys. The status should change.

Check it worked: click Browse Files. You should see a list of your files.

03

Start your GPU session

Screenshots are from the Tapis tutorial, so they show a different app and different values. Use the values written here: app jupyter-hpc-native, queue gh, allocation TRA25008, reservation inspireGPU.

a. Pick the app

Click Apps, click jupyter-hpc-native, then click Submit Job.

b. Choose the guided launcher

Click Guided job launcher (v2).

c. Job Basics

Click Continue at the bottom left.

d. Execution Options

Job Type: Batch · Batch Logical Queue: gh. Expand Queue Parameters and set nodes to 1 and max minutes to 100. Then Continue.

e. File Inputs, Job Arguments, Environment Variables

Click Continue on each. Don't change anything.

f. Scheduler Options

Expand TACC Resource Allocation. After -A, type a space and TRA25008.
Expand Reservation Name. After --reservation, type a space and inspireGPU. Then Continue.

g. Submit

On Archiving, click Continue. On Review & Submit, click Submit Job. When you see the confirmation, close the window.

04

Open Jupyter

Click Jobs in the left panel and click your job. Wait until it shows the running icon. When the session box appears, click the button to open Jupyter.

No button? Open the output file tapisjob.out, copy the address after JUPYTER_URL, and open it in a new tab.

05

Get the workshop

In Jupyter, click File → New → Terminal and type:

cd $WORK
git clone https://github.com/ashleyscruse/hpc-finetune-workshop

Then open hpc-finetune-workshop/notebooks/01-fine-tune.ipynb.

06

Run it

Click a cell and press Shift + Enter. Go top to bottom. A cell is done when the [*] beside it turns into a number.

WRITE THESE DOWN  Your accuracy after training and how many seconds training took. You need both this afternoon.
07

Before lunch: end your session

In the portal, open your job and cancel it. This frees your GPU for the afternoon. Your results are saved.
